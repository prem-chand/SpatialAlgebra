3. inline RobotModel makeTwoLinkWith90DegreeRotation()
   - 2 links: link0 (parent=-1, Z-revolute, mass=1, COM=Zero, name="link0")
   - link1: parent=0, Z-revolute, mass=1, COM=Zero,
     parentToJoint with 90° Z rotation (AngleAxisd(M_PI_2, UnitZ())) AND 1m X translation,
     name="link1"

     in topRightCorner. bottomRow = [0,0,0,1]

     PluckerTransformUsage test."

Rotations are via AngleAxisd.matrix() placed in parentToJoint.topLeftCorner<3,3>().
parentToJoint starts as Eigen::Matrix4d::Identity() then modified in place.
