<objective>
Add gravity support to both forward dynamics (ABA) and inverse dynamics (RNEA) via optional `gravity` parameter, defaulting to zero for backward compatibility. Gravity is implemented as base link acceleration per Featherstone formulation (D-07, D-08). The gravity vector is expressed in **world/base coordinates** (not local body frames) — this convention is explicitly documented.

**Purpose:** Enable robots to compute dynamics under gravity — essential for all practical robotics applications. Addresses review concerns HIGH #2 (sign convention errors) and HIGH #3 (frame ambiguity: is gravity in world or local frame coordinates?).
**Output:** Gravity-aware computeAccelerations() and computeTorques() that accept `Vector3d gravity` param with zero default, with explicit frame convention documentation.
</objective>
