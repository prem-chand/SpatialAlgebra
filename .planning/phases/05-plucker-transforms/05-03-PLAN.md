    auto M = Ia.getM();
    auto H = Ia.getH();
    auto Inertia = Ia.getInertia();

}

<!-- Mathematical background -->
**Articulated Body Inertia Transform (Featherstone Eq 7.16):**
For I'a = X * Ia * X^T:
- M' = R * M * R^T
- H' = R * (H - M * r̂) * R^T
- I' = R * (I - H^T * r̂ - r̂ * H + r̂ * M * r̂) * R^T

**Inverse ABI Transform:**
For I'a = X^(-1) * Ia * X^(-T):
- M' = R^T * M * R
- H' = R^T * (H + M * r̂) * R
- I' = R^T * (I + H^T * r̂ + r̂ * H + r̂ * M * r̂) * R
