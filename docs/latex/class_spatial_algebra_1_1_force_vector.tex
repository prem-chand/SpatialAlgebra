\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Class representing spatial force vectors (wrenches) 

The \doxylink{class_spatial_algebra_1_1_force_vector}{Force\+Vector} class specializes \doxylink{class_spatial_algebra_1_1_spatial_vector}{Spatial\+Vector} for representing force quantities in spatial algebra. It provides type-\/safe operations specific to force vectors and ensures correct geometric interpretation.

Key Concepts\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Force vectors form a vector space
\item They transform contravariantly under coordinate changes
\item The cross product represents force transformation in rigid bodies
\item Dot product with motion vectors yields power
\end{DoxyItemize}

Common Applications\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Representing joint forces in articulated systems
\item Describing external loads on rigid bodies
\item Force propagation through kinematic chains
\end{DoxyItemize}

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Force vectors and motion vectors are dual to each other, meaning they transform differently under coordinate changes and their dot product yields physically meaningful power. 
\end{DoxyNote}
